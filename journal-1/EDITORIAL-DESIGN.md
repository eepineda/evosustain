# Evoford Journal — Premium Editorial Direction

The redesign follows contemporary digital-editorial principles: strong typographic hierarchy, structured/asymmetric grids, deliberate whitespace, restrained color, full-width imagery, pull-quote/article rhythm and responsive mobile-first behavior. Current 2026 editorial design references emphasize typography as a primary system, grid structure, white space and responsive storytelling rather than card-heavy SaaS layouts. See the research notes used for this direction in the project README.

This is an original Evoford Journal visual identity, not a reproduction of any third-party publication.
