Sustainability programmes increasingly depend on the quality of the information moving through an organisation. The operational question is no longer only what should change, but how teams measure, coordinate and improve that change.

## From targets to operating systems

Digital workflows can connect collection, logistics, energy, procurement and reporting into a shared operational picture. That creates a clearer path between an environmental target and the activity required to reach it.

> The useful metric is the one that changes a decision.

For organisations building this capability, the next stage is less about adding another dashboard and more about creating reliable information flows between people, software and physical operations.
